## 1. Overview
Mobilis ("we", "our", or "the app") provides vehicle rental, fleet management, and mobility solutions. This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is collected, used, and protected when using the Mobilis mobile app and web platform.

---

## 2. Information We Collect
To provide reliable vehicle rental operations, we collect the following data:
- **Profile & Contact Details:** Name, email address, phone number, and account credentials.
- **Identity Verification Documents:** Driver's licenses and government IDs to verify driver eligibility.
- **Location Information:** Precise and approximate location to provide vehicle pickup discovery, route navigation, and rental tracking.
- **Camera & Photos:** Used solely for capturing vehicle condition inspection photos and uploading verification documents.
- **Transactions & Bookings:** Reservation details, receipts, payment histories, and security deposit statuses.

---

## 3. How Data is Used
Collected information is used strictly to:
- Facilitate car rental bookings and vehicle handovers.
- Verify driver identity and prevent fraud.
- Maintain passenger and vehicle safety during active rental trips.
- Deliver push notifications and booking updates.
- Securely process payments, security deposits, and refunds.

---

## 4. Data Sharing & Security
- We do not sell or rent user data.
- Sensitive information (such as live trip locations and driver's licenses) is restricted to authorized personnel.
- All communications and database transactions are secured using standard encryption via Supabase and Firebase.

---

## 5. Account and Data Deletion
Users have the right to request access, correction, or complete deletion of their account and personal data at any time via the application settings or by contacting our team.
