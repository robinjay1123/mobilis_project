Mobilis Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 8, 2026 | Last Updated: October 2026

Operated by Pangasinan State University - PSDC (Pangasinan Software Development Center)

1. Overview

Mobilis ("we", "our", or "the app") provides vehicle rental, fleet management, and mobility solutions. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use our mobile application and web portal.

2. Information We Collect

To provide safe and reliable vehicle rental services, we collect the following types of data:

  • Account & Profile Information: Name, email address, phone number, and account credentials.
  • Identity Verification Data: Driver's license details and government-issued identification strictly for verifying eligibility to operate and rent vehicles.
  • Location Information: Precise and approximate location data when the app is in use to provide nearby vehicle discovery, trip navigation, and safety tracking during active rental trips.
  • Camera & Photo Access: Camera access is used solely when capturing vehicle condition photos (inspections before/after rental) and uploading identity verification documents.
  • Transaction & Booking Details: Vehicle reservations, payment receipts, rental history, and security deposit statuses.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data strictly for service operation, including:

  • Facilitating car rental reservations and key exchanges.
  • Verifying driver qualifications and preventing identity fraud.
  • Ensuring passenger and vehicle safety through active trip tracking during rentals.
  • Sending important booking confirmations, rental updates, and push notifications.
  • Processing payments, refunds, and security deposits securely.

4. Data Sharing & Confidentiality

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Access to your sensitive information (such as live trip locations and identity documents) is strictly limited to authorized personnel responsible for verification, customer support, and emergency response.

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard encryption and security measures via Supabase and Firebase. We retain personal and rental data only as long as necessary to fulfill service operations, legal obligations, and dispute resolution.

6. Your Rights & Account Deletion

Users have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of their personal data. You may request account and data deletion at any time by contacting our support team or through the app settings.
